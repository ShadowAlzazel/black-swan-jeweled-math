# Third-party material

## Hat substitution source and derived geometry

The hat substitution implementation comes from [isohedral/hatviz](https://github.com/isohedral/hatviz), linked by the [authors' project page](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~csk/hat/). Its original BSD-3-Clause license and copyright notices are retained at:

- `experiments/02_tiling_jewels/tiling_research/LICENSE`
- `experiments/03_golden_sweep/HAT_SOURCE_LICENSE`
- `experiments/03_golden_sweep/tiling_research/LICENSE`

The original JavaScript files remain in `experiments/02_tiling_jewels/tiling_research/`. Their source revisions are recorded in the geometry JSON and the consolidation provenance.

The pinwheel implementation is an explicit affine subdivision described in its accompanying notes. Mathematical background and the source of that construction are linked in the experiment README.
