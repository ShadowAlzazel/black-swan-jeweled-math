# Where the ideas enter

The schedules are deliberately creative, prespecified heuristics. Their ingredients have different mathematical roles; combining names does not combine their theorems into a SAT guarantee.

| Earlier idea | Concrete role in this experiment |
| --- | --- |
| 3SAT and reductions | Exact CNF remains authoritative. The engine uses unit propagation and exhaustive two-branch DPLL when heuristic repair does not finish. Long clauses in pigeonhole examples receive a satisfiability-preserving auxiliary-variable chain. |
| The jewel's zeros | Every clause weight is positive, so zero weighted energy means exactly zero violated clauses. |
| Golden ratio and irrational rotations | Golden phase increments perturb geometry-based variable and clause priorities. Silver ratio, e, and zeta(2) provide alternative scales. |
| Hats, pinwheels, quasicrystals | Actual saved substitution/cut-and-project positions supply features; shared edges supply optional diffusion neighbors. |
| Two complex planes | Exponential, squaring, and Möbius images turn tile positions into ordering features. Variable identities are retained even if the geometric images collide. |
| Euler's identity | Rotations use the complex exponential; the 3-4-1 identity supplies a positive adaptive boost, normalized over violated clauses. |
| Basel / pi²/6 | zeta(2) supplies a quasiperiodic phase scale; no special SAT property is presumed. |
| Feigenbaum | A bounded critical orbit at the computed period-512 center supplies deterministic phases. Alpha scales spatial features and delta supplies a phase increment; this use does not assert a new universality law. |
| Zeta / RH / prime spectra | Ten previously computed critical-line ordinates enter cos(gamma*log(prime)+phase) features. This finite construction does not require global RH and is not an operator proving RH. |
| Eigenvectors and cycle kernels | Deflated diffusion approximates two low modes of the actual variable-interaction Laplacian. Exact XOR recognition separately exposes a binary linear system, with rank, nullspace, and contradiction certificates. |
| Collatz and Fourier modes | Exactly 64 parity steps and eight discrete Fourier coefficients provide a finite schedule. The computation makes no assumption about eventual Collatz termination. |
| Randomness and look-alike controls | Seeded phases, random escape, and a shuffled cocktail control test attachment sensitivity. The latter preserves score/weight multisets and graph isomorphism while changing their logical attachment. |
| Adaptive landscape | Bounded repair changes positive clause penalties at traps; complete DPLL follows if repair fails. Diffusion uses nonnegative neighbor averages. |

The spectral iteration is a feature generator, not an exact diagonalization claim. Its setup, geometry, trigonometry, and kernel work have computational costs. The engine records front-end and exact-search work separately; node counts are not total runtime.

Recognized XOR systems can be solved by Gaussian elimination, an established polynomial algorithm for that restricted structure. General OR clauses still require the exact fallback. A full binary search tree remains an exponential worst-case possibility. No sound geometric state-compression rule or polynomial bound for arbitrary 3SAT is provided.
